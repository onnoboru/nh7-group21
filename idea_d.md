who: kids aged 6-12

what: an AI powered application that acts as a conduit to bridge intergenerational gaps within the household, especially targeting children in their adolescence to inculcate the values of being family oriented and building meaningful relationships

where: on their personal and/or home devices 

when: whenever convenient for the users ie when they are together and able to interact in-person 

why: elderly loneliness has been an increasingly prevalent issue due to the advent of technology, intrahousehold relationships become strained if children do not build the habit of wanting to have a close relationship with their family members from young

how: 
- develop an app that offers different means as well as incentives for children to interact with their household members, especially the elderly like their grandparents 
- ⁠gameifying/providing activities for children to do with their grandparents such that it makes interactions fun and enjoyable which would (hopefully) act as an impetus for continual relationship building and maintenance 
- ⁠for elderly that do not stay in the same household as their grand children or just lack the means to interact with/dont have children, app offers an online community likened to a “digital kampung" that is able to link elderly up with children who wish to partake in activities but also might not have the means of interacting with their grandparents
- ⁠as a side benefit, app could also serve as an early builder for the foundation of the children's communication skills like dillon mentioned
