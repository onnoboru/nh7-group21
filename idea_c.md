Who: Children aged 6–15 and older adults they already know or meet through a family-approved trusted circle.

What: A hybrid app for short intergenerational activities, played face to face on one device or remotely with an approved connection.

Where and when: At home, in community spaces, or online after school and on weekends.

Why: To turn occasional contact into lasting relationships, reduce loneliness safely, broaden children’s horizons, and build communication and negotiation skills they may not develop at school.

How: Two people choose shared interests, play a guided game or conversation, leave separate feedback, and grow a shared journey over time. The app suggests the next activity from explicit ratings and choices, without listening to private conversations. Families can later invite trusted people, schedule remote sessions, and join moderated interest circles. Points celebrate time spent together and can eventually unlock partner rewards.
