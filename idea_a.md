who: kids aged 12+ for online version, kids aged 6-12 for home version or with parental supervision and grandparents above retirement age

what: AI-Powered application that brings youth and seniors with common interests together & aims to build a digital community 

where: on their phones/electronic devices

when: after school hours/weekends 

why: combat loneliness in an ageing society

how: 
1. Enquires interests from users 
2. ⁠matches seniors w a child that has any overlapping interest (or users can choose to play in house game)
3. ⁠sets up a call and provides a game, discussion topic, show/ movie clip in the area of interest.
4. ⁠Gets a review of the activity from both users and offers the ability to add each other as friends on the app
5. ⁠Recommends users to join a interest group on the app based on their reviews 
6. ⁠Rewards users with points that they can claim vouchers from partnering companies
